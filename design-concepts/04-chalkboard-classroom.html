Lesson 02 · Stakeholder language

Push back,
without pushing away.

Objective: challenge a request with evidence, protect the relationship, and land on a measurable decision.

The response sequence

1Acknowledge the stakeholder’s goal.
2Name the trade-off with specific evidence.
3Push back on the method, not the person.
4Offer a smaller, testable alternative.
5Close with the decision and result.

“I understood why speed mattered. I pushed back on a full launch because the data showed a retention risk, and proposed a two-week pilot instead.”

Board exercise

Complete the sentence in natural interview English:

“I didn’t simply disagree. I ______ on the launch plan and explained the trade-off.”

Hint: use today’s target phrase in the past tense.
